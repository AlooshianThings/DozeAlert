Nodding off? Doze Alert has your back.

It watches you through your webcam and gives you a loud nudge when your eyes close or your head drops. Made for late-night study sessions and long drives.

Try it out How does it work?

Everything runs in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

You never notice the moment you drift off.

Usually you only find out when your head jerks up. Doze Alert spots it a moment earlier.

For students

We have all dozed off over a book. A quick nudge lets you stand up, splash some water on your face, and get back to it.

For drivers

A few seconds of sleep behind the wheel is dangerous. Doze Alert is a reminder to pull over and rest. It's a student project, not a certified safety device.

So how does it work?

  1. Your camera watches

    Frames from your webcam are read right inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

  2. Two models decide

    One looks at your eyes and yawns (Awake, Eyes Closed, Yawning, Nobody). The other looks at your head position (Awake, Drowsy, Nobody). Both were trained with Google Teachable Machine.

  3. The alarm wakes you

    When both models agree you look drowsy for a few seconds (or one model for longer), a loud siren, a spoken wake-up, and a full-screen warning kick in. Sit up and it stops.

Give it a go

Sit facing the camera with your face well lit, press start, then close your eyes and let your head drop for a few seconds to see what happens.

The camera is off. Press Start checking to begin.

Camera off

Turn your volume up first. A web page can't change your phone's volume or silent mode, so press "Test alarm sound" to check you can hear it. Switch off silent mode and Do Not Disturb while checking.

Your video is processed inside this browser and never uploaded. On a phone, the screen stays awake while checking.

Ready when you are

Press Start checking and allow the camera.

Alarm charge

Fills while you look drowsy. At 100% the alarm goes off.
